Why the warning — and why it’s safe

Windows and macOS warn about any app that isn’t enrolled in their paid publisher programs. It is not a virus alert. ES runs only on your computer: nothing is uploaded, your TopstepX login stays on your machine.

What the app connects to

  • TopstepX — your own account, for orders and data
  • Whop — to check your license key
  • fkfutures.com — to check for updates

Nothing else. No tracking, no analytics.
